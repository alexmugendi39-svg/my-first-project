About Me - My Hockey Journey 🏑

My name is Alex Mugendi, a passionate teacher and a dedicated hockey player and coach from Isiolo/Meru. My love for hockey has shaped my discipline, teamwork, and leadership both on and off the field.

  • 2017: Started playing hockey after joining Form One - where my journey began.
  • 2020: Together with my fellow teammates, we represented our school at the County Games and emerged Position 3.
  • 2024: Won the Eastern Regional Hockey Tournament and qualified for the Nationals held at Machakos TTC.
  • 2026: Led the Blinders Hockey Team as the Assistant Captain, won the Eastern Regionals and qualified for Nationals at Homa Bay where we emerged Position 3 National-wide.

Through hockey, I have learned resilience, leadership, and the power of teamwork. Currently, I serve as a player and coach for Blinders Hockey Club Meru, nurturing the next generation of talents while also serving as a CBC teacher sharing educational resources.
