🏑 Hockey Achievements

Started playing hockey in 2017 after joining Form 1 through my best friend. In 2020, I represented Kanyakine High at County level and finished Position 3.

In 2024 I joined Meru Teachers College. In 2025, I represented my College in Eastern Regional Hockey Tournament held at Igoji TTC and qualified for Nationals at Machakos TTC, Nairobi Region.

In 2026 as Assistant Captain, I led Blinders Hockey Team again in Eastern Regional Tournament and qualified for Nationals at Asumbi TTC, Nyanza Region, Homabay County where we emerged Position 3 National-wide, earning Certificate of Merit.

Currently playing for Blinders Hockey Team Meru, and coaching my fellow teammates. Above all, continuing nurturing young minds in Education as a DPTE teacher.
